# XXI Congress of the Academy after the Diploma in Warsaw ENG

On March 14th and 15th, we had the pleasure of participating in the 21st Congress of the Academy of Veterinary Medicine in Warsaw.

We would like to thank the organizers for the professional preparation of the congress, which attracted such a large crowd of veterinarians from all over Poland.

In a wonderful atmosphere, we had the opportunity to meet with a large group of veterinarians at our stand, where we were able to share and share knowledge about our new and updated products, and remind them about other products.

Thanks to this, we were able to establish new contacts, convince veterinarians to try new items from our extensive range, and collect numerous orders, even encouragingly, for new products that will not be available until April.

Thank you all again, and see you soon 🙂
