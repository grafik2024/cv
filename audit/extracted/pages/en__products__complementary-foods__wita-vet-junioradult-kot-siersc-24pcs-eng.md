## purpose

Supplementary feed recommended for dry, rough, matt and damaged coat, in case of hair loss, shedding and in preparation for animal shows. Feed ingredients help to regain skin tone, coat color and shine, regulate sebum production and promote skin hydration. When preparing cats for shows, start administating the feed 3 weeks before the date.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food:

-

**cats** with body weight up to 4 kg 1 tablet / day

-

**cats** with body weight over 4 kg 2 tablets / day

Administer for 8-10 days in a month. When preparing dogs for a show, start 3 weeks before the show.

The product contains protein of animal origin. It can be fed to animals other than ruminants.

###   Indications

 Skin and coat regenerator Shine coat. Healthy skin.

###   for which animals

 For cats.

 Ingredients: Brewers’ yeast (from Saccharomyces cerevisiae), animal by-products (pulverized swine liver), crude lecithins.
