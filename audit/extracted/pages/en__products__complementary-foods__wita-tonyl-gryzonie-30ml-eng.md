## purpose

**WITA TONYL** provides vitamins necessary to meet nutritional requirements of small animals. The product is intended to be used during growth, pregnancy and nursing. It has a beneficial influence on older, tired or convalescing animals and in case of poor appetite. Hydrolysed liver extract provides B vitamins and haematopoietic agents. Due to the content of amino acids, such as isoleucine, leucine, methionine, phenylalanine, threonine, valine, WITA TONYL positively affects functioning of the body. Vitamins improve metabolism, support immune processes of the body, aid regeneration of epithelium and skin, participate in bone mineralization and regulate calcium and phosphorus balance.

###   Use

 Mix with food:

-

**rabbits** 3-5 drops / day

-

**guinea pigs**, hamsters, mice 1-2 drops / day

-

**turtles** 1 drop / week / 100 g

###   Indications

 Growth. Pregnancy. Nursing. Convalescence.

 For rabbits, turtles, guinea pigs, hamsters, mice
