## purpose

HEPACHOL syrup positively affects functioning of the digestive system, aids functioning of the liver. Ingredients of the feed stimulate intestinal peristalsis and bile production as well as secretion of digestive enzymes. This product is recommended in case of overfeeding, decreased appetite, weakness, constipation, vomiting.

###   Use

 Mix with food or water:

-

- **Horses and adult cattle**:

-5ml/animal/day for 4 months;

-

– as food supplement during weakness – 60 ml / animal for 7 days;

- **Calves**:

– from the 1st 

-

– as food supplement during weakness – 30 ml / animal for 5 days. Administer twice a day.

- **Sheep, goats**:

– adult animals: 15-20 ml / animal / day for 5-7 days

– lambs and goat kids: 5-10 ml / animal / day for 5-7 days month of life until slaughter – 2 ml /animal / day

- **Pigs**:

– weaning piglets-1,5ml in 1 l of water or 1ml/animal/day for 7days

– sows- 50 ml / animal / day for 5 days before and after the birth

– as food supplement during weakness:

piglets- 3-5 ml / animal / day

porkers- 15 ml / animal / day for 5 days

sows- 50 ml / animal / day for 5 days

- **Broiler chickens**, hens, ducks, geese, turkeys, rabbits:

1-2 ml in 1 l of water for 5days

– fattening of geese: 4 ml / bird / day for 5 days in the middle of the fattening period
