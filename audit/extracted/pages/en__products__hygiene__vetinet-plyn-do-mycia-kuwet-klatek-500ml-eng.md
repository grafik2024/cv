## PURPOSE

**VETINET **effectively eliminates unpleasant odours. Readily removes organic and inorganic contaminants. Presents excellent cleaning properties. Recommended for use on litter boxes, kennels, pens, travel carriers and other pet accessories.

###   Use

 Remove all loose objects from cleaned area. Spray the product all over the surface. Leave on for several minutes. Wipe off remaining product with a damp cloth, Repeat if needed.

###   Indications

 Hygienic cleaning liquid for litter boxes, kennels and other pet accessories.
