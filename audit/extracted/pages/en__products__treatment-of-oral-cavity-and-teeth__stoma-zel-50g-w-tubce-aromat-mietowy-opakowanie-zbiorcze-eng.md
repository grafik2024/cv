## purpose

**STOMA-GEL** is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic intended for teeth care. An addition of chlorhexidine prevents the development of undesirable bacterial microflora in the oral cavity, eliminates bad breath and keeps the teeth and gums clean. Due to the added flavouring, pets easily tolerate its application. Abrasives prevent the development of tartar and, when combined with feed particles, form an emulsion which removes it from the tooth surface.

###   Use

 1-2 time(s) a week or every day, depending on the condition of teeth. Apply the paste using a toothbrush, finger or gauze. Rub in gently. Do not rinse.

###   Indications

 Oral hygiene.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Ingredients
