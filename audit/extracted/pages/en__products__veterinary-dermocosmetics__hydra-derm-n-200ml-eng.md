## purpose

**Hydra-Derm N** is a veterinary dermocosmetic which helps to maintain the water and lipid balance in case of dry skin or dandruff (dry seborrhea). It demonstrates water retention properties preventing skin from drying out.

###   Use

 1) rinse: dilute 20 ml of liquid in 1 litre of water. Apply on skin and coat. Massage and dry. 2) spray: dilute with water at a ratio of 1:2. Apply on skin and coat once a day. Work in and dry.

###   Indications

 General or local moisturization of dry or flaky skin. Skin softening in case of local keratosis (elbows, ankle joints, etc.).

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Do not apply to wounds. Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 AQUA, GLICERIN, UREA, POLYSORBATE 20, PARFUM, LACTIC ACIDS, DMDM HYDANTOIN, METHYLCHLOROISOTHIAZOLINONE, METHYLISOTHIAZOLINONE
