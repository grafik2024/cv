## purpose

Complementary feed to support the proper functioning of the joints. Recommended for cats with bone and joint problems, such as dysplasia, degenerative changes or damage after injury. The ingredients contained in the product – Chondroitin, Glucosamine, Devil’s Claw, Fish Collagen, Methylsulfonylmethane (MSM) and Hyaluronic Acid – strengthen the function of the joints and support their mobility and flexibility. Rosehip extract provides vitamin C, which is involved in the synthesis of joint cartilage collagen.
