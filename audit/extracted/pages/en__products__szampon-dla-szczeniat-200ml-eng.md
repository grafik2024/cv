## purpose

The shampoo has very good cleansing properties and is easy to rinse. The almond oil makes it easily tolerated by puppies. It makes the coat soft and shiny.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Wet the puppy’s coat and skin thoroughly with warm water, pour on the shampoo (avoiding the eyes), spread evenly, massage in and then rinse off. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the coat and skin for several minutes. Rinse off with lukewarm water, dry and brush.

###   Indications

 Coat and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 Sweet almond oil, delicate washing base, moisturisers, natural fragrances
