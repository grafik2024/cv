## purpose

Nutrikard contains ingredients that improve cardiovascular system function. Taurine aids cardiac muscle contraction. Co-enzyme Q10 supplies energy needed for efficient heart functioning. Substances contained in japanese knotweed root and olive leaves protect circulatory system, boost blood flow in coronary blood vessels and regulate blood pressure. The feed is a tasty treat thus pets consume it willingly. Indications: Complementary feed for dogs and cats that offers suport for cardiovascular system. Substances contained in the feed positively boost cardiovascular system function. Taurine is a metabolic transmitter, takes part in body detox and aids cardiac muscle contraction. Co-enzyme Q10 that originates from Saccharomyces cerevisiae yeast delivers energy needed for efficient heart function and reduces oxidative stress caused by free radicals. Trans-resveratrol from japanse knotweed has antioxidative properties, improves microcirculation in circulatory system helps to prevent formation of clots in blood vessels. Hydroxytyrosol from olive leaves prevents aggregation of platelets, promotes healthy blood flow in coronary system and has beneficial effect on blood pressure.
