## purpose

**WITAZIUM **contains taurine, which supports the efficient functioning of the lens and retina. Turmeric extract protects the organ of vision, exerting an anti-inflammatory effect. Carotenoids (lutein and zeaxanthin) maintain and improve vision. Superoxide dismutase activates natural processes that protect the eye. Vitamins E and C and oligo-elements (zinc, selenium) improve metabolic processes in the eye.

**Taurine** is found in large amounts in the lens and retina of the eye. It protects the eyes from harmful UV radiation.

**Tagetes erecta **contains **lutein **and** zeaxanthin**, which are natural antioxidants. They are present in large quantities in the retina and lens of the eye. They absorb blue light, which is the most harmful to the eyes. Lutein improves vision.

**Concentrated melon juice **is a naturally good source of superoxide dismutase, which supports natural processes that inhibit the production of free radicals. It activates substances that halt or slow down oxidation processes.

 

**Turmeric **contains polyphenol, which protects the eyes, exerting a multidirectional anti-inflammatory effect. It reduces the production of free radicals through antioxidant reactions.

 

**Vitamin C **protects cells from harmful metabolic products. It participates in the synthesis of hormones.

**Vitamin E** is involved in the metabolism of unsaturated fatty acids, preventing the formation of free radicals.

**Zinc** is involved in the conversion of vitamin A into light-absorbing pigment.

**Selenium** is essential for normal enzymatic activity in the elimination of toxic products formed in the body.
