## PURPOSE

Feed supplement for dogs and cats designed to restore B12 reserve in the liver. Its deficiency is often observed in pancreatic insufficiency, chronic inflammatory bowel disease, liver and bile ducts inflammation.

###   USE

 Feed directly or crushed and mixed into regular feed.

– **up to 10 kg of body weight** – 1/2 tab. / day

– **from 10 to 20 kg of body weight** – 1 tab. / day

– **over 20 kg bodyweight** – 2 tab. / day.

Tablets are very palatable for animals, thus pets willingy accept the suplement. 

**Recommended usage period**: 3 months. Following this period consult a veterinarian and arrange vitamin B12 level test in order to establish maintenance dose.

###   INDICATIONS

 Improves liver, pancreas and bowel function

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For dogs and cats.
