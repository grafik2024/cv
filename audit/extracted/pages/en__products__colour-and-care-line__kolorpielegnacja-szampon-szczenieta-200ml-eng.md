## purpose

The ingredients of the shampoo help to clean your dog and ensure easy rinsing. The skin and coat of your dog is cleaned thoroughly and easy to brush. The almond tree oil makes the shampoo perfect for puppies. The coat becomes soft and shiny. Allantoin soothes skin irritation. The skin is protected against skin injuries including minor cuts, damage, scrapes or sunburns. The shampoo does not cause allergic reactions.

###   Use

 For external use only. Shake well before use. Wet the coat, apply the shampoo, lather, wash and rinse. Repeat the action, leaving the shampoo on the fur and skin for a few minutes. Rinse with lukewarm water, dry and brush. After washing use the hydrating fluid HYDRA-DERM N.

###   Indications

 Cleaning the skin and coat of puppies.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.
