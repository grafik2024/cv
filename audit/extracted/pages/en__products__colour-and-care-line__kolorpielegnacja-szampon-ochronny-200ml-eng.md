## purpose

The preparation helps protect dogs and cats against fleas, ticks and other insects. It also has very good cleansing properties.

###   Use

 For external use only. Shake well before use. Wet the coat, apply the shampoo, lather, wash and rinse. Repeat the action, leaving the shampoo on the fur and skin for a few minutes. Rinse with lukewarm water, dry and brush. After washing use the hydrating fluid HYDRA-DERM N.

###   Indications

 Coat and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with animal's eyes. If product accidently gets into eyes, rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Do not use on dogs and cats below 3 months of age or on pregnant or nursing animals. To minimise the risk of the animal getting fleas or ticks, change its bed periodically. Store in original container, keep away from children.
