# effective

## purpose

**ALU-DERM** is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic to be used on damaged skin. The product ingredients regulate microflora and aid its regeneration. **ALU-DERM** has astringent properties.

###   Use

 Use on damaged skin. Spray from the distance of 15-20 cm until the damaged area is completely covered. Hold the container in the upright position. Repeat 2-3 times a day or when needed. Shake well before use.

###   Indications

 Helps to regenerate damaged skin

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes. Use away from open flames (explosion hazard) and in a well ventilated area. Do not pierce. Do not heat. Pressurized metal container. Do not smoke while using the product. Do not inhale the aerosol.

###   Ingredients

 CI 77000, ISOBUTYL METHACRYLATE, MEK
