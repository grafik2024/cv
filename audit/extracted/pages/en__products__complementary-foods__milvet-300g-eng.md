## purpose

**MILVET** is a complete milk replacer, which perfectly replaces natural breast milk. It provides easily digestible protein and easily assimilated fat. Feeding milk replacer to bitches and mother cats suckling their young can increase production of their own milk. It provides puppies and kittens with necessary energy, vitamins and building material. MILVET satisfies mineral and vitamin requirements of growing animals. It is very well assimilated and can be fed right after birth. It is intended for puppies and kittens reared without mothers, in case of insufficient amounts of milk produced by the mother, as a food supplement during the weaning process, during the period of convalescence or increased effort (e.g. hunting).

###   Use

 Determine the portion of the preparation according to the table on the packaging. Pour one portion of the preparation into the bottle, then add two portions of boiled water at about 60 ° C - 1: 2 ratio. Mix vigorously. Cool to 37 ° C. For the first 15 days of life, feed through a teat and then switch to a bowl. After the 4th week of life, during the gradual transition to solid feed, add powder to the feed.
