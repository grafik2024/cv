## purpose

**Ektozin** is a veterinary dermocosmetic, which alleviates symptoms associated with the irritation of the conjunctiva and supports the natural moisturising of the eyes and eyelids. It helps the removal of contaminations from the conjunctival sac. The ectoin contained in the product is a naturally existing substance. Its objective is to protect cells from the harmful effects of external factors, sooth inflammations and to ensure the protection of epithelial cells.

###   Use

 Moisturise the eye with a saturated compress or apply several drops of the fluid into the conjunctival sac. Now remove any redundant fluid. Rinse the eyelids as well. Apply as needed, even every day.

###   Indications

 Eye and eyelid hygiene.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Discontinue use in case of intolerance.

###   Ingredients

 Aqua, Centaurea Cyanus Floral Water, Ectoin, Sodium Chloride, Benzalkonium Chloride, Sodium Methylparaben, Phosphoric Acid
