# effective

## purpose

**FLORANTYNOL** is intended for ornamental birds as a food or water supplement. It provides vitamins necessary to meet nutritional requirements of birds. The feed ingredients boost the growing process of young birds, influence the plumage, vitality and breeding. Hydrolysed liver extract provides iron as well as vitamins A, D, K and small amounts of B and C vitamins. Vitamins influence metabolism, support immune processes of the body, affect regeneration of epithelium, participate in bone mineralization and regulate calcium and phosphorus balance. The product has a beneficial influence on convalescing birds and birds with poor appetite.

###   Use

 2 drops of the feed/ 5 ml of water (1 teaspoon) and soak 30 g of grains (35 drops / 75 ml of water and soak 500 g of grains). Administer1-2 time(s) a week. 1 ml contains 20 drops. The product contains protein of animal origin. It can be fed to animals other than ruminants

###   INDICATIONS

 Growth. Plumage. Vitality. Reproduction.

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For ornamental birds.

###   Ingredients

 Composition: by-products of animal origin (from pigs), propylene glycol Analytical constituents: crude protein 20,2%, crude ash 0,1%, crude oils and fats 3,1%, crude fibre 0%, calcium 0,01%, phosphorus 0,33%, sodium 0,08, %lysine 0%, methionine 0%, humidity 71,6% Additives in 1 l: vitamins and provitamins: E672 vitamin A 10 000 000 IU, E671 vitamin D3 1 000 000 IU, 3a700 vitamin E 30 000 IU, vitamin B1 1800 mg, vitamin B2 2000 mg, 3a831 vitamin B6 6000 mg, nicotinic acid 30 000 mg, calcium D-pantothenate 6000 mg, vitamin B12 30 mg, emulsifying and stabilizing agents: E484 glyceryl polyethyleneglycol ricinoleate, preservatives: E200 sorbic acid 1,5g, E330 citric acid 0,5g
