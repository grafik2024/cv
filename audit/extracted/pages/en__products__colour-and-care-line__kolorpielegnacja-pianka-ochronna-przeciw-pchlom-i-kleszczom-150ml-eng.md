## purpose

The preparation helps to protect dogs and cats from fleas, ticks and other insects.

###   Use

 Brush coat in opposite direction to the growth direction of the hairs. Apply 1 or 2 walnut-sized portions of foam to the palm of your hand, apply to coat and rub into skin in the direction opposite to the growth direction of the hairs. Repeat application until body surface is completely covered. Let dry naturally and brush.

###   Indications

 For coat and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact of the foam with eyes. Not intended for dogs and cats under 1 year of age. Note: in order to obtain the maximum effect of the preparation, you should take your dog for a walk after using it. In order to reduce the risk of fleas and ticks infestation, it is necessary to replace the pet bed periodically and regularly.
