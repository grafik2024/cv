## purpose

Spray for dogs and cats to maintain good oral and dental hygiene. Natural rockrose and peppermint extracts support the healthy bacterial microflora of the oral cavity and thus ensure fresh breath.

###   Use

 Shake the bottle before use. Spray on your pet’s teeth and gums. The teeth can also be cleaned with a gauze pad moistened with the product.
