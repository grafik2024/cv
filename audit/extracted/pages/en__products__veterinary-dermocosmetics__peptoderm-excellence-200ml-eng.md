## PROPERTIES

**Peptoderm excellence **is a veterinary dermocosmetic which regulates microflora of the skin, stabilses its pH and prevents drying by the formation of a lipid layer. Innovative formula utilises functional synergy of AMP antimicrobial peptide, chlorhexidine and terpenin-4-ol in order to eliminate harmful microorganisms that cause a number of skin conditions. Additionally, this solution prevents formation of antimicrobial resistance.

###   USE

 Carefully brush the coat before bath. Wet the coat and the skin with warm water, apply the shampoo over the whole body and spread carefully. Massage until foam is formed, then rinse. Repeat the actions. Allow the shampoo to remain on the coat and the skin for few minutes and rinse thoroughly with warm water. Frequency of use: use 2-3 times per week or according to needs.

###   INDICATIONS

 Skin and coat care in treatment of bacterial inflammation of the skin. Maintaining natural skin microflora. Seborrhea.

###   PRECAUTIONS

 Avoid contact with eyes.
