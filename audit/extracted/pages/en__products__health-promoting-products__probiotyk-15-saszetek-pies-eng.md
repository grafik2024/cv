## purpose

A probiotic is a combination of health-promoting microorganisms naturally FOUND in the gastrointestinal tract and vitamin, mineral, amino acid concentrate. Biological protection against pathogenic strains of bacteria (e.g.: Escherichia coli, Salmonella, Mycobacterium, Shigella, Bacteroides, Proteus, Streptococcus, Clostridium, Pseudomonas, Vibrio), viruses (rotavirus and others). 1 sachet of probiotic contains 7.2 x 109 live microorganisms.

###   Use

 Suitable for all breeds and age groups of dogs. The preparation is administered in food or drinking water:

- dogs up to 5 kg body weight – ½ sachet every 3 days,

- dogs from 5 to 20 kg body weight – 1 sachet every 3 days,

- dogs above 20 kg body weight – 2 sachets every 3 days

###   Indications

 Use during: antibiotic therapy, diarrhoea, constipation, digestive disorders, deworming, infectious diseases, immunodeficiency, stress, unpleasant oral odour.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.
