## PURPOSE

Dry & Clean foam is suitable for cleaning animal coat and skin without using water. It may be used during travel or before an exhibition. It proves really helpful when removing soiling from the skin and coat after veterinary treatment. Dry & Clean foam helps to thoroughly clean and moisturize the skin and coat. It does not cause irritation. The coat remains soft, shiny and easy to brush.

###   Use

 Shake before use. Apply the foam onto a wet glove and gently massage onto the coat. Work in gradually, and keep adding the shampoo until the skin and coat are clean. Wipe the coat with a towel and brush.

###   INDICATIONS

 Coat and skin hygiene.

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For dogs, cats and other animals.

###   PRECAUTIONS

 Avoid contact with eyes.
