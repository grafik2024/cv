## properties

**Ektovet **shampoo, enriched with Ophiopogon japonicus and Ectoin, is a veterinary dermocosmetic intended for the care of dry and sensitive skin and the coat of dogs and cats. Recommended for skin prone to allergies, dryness and loss of elasticity. Thanks to Ectoin, which binds water, it intensely moisturises and helps protect against transepidermal water loss (TEWL). It supports the regeneration of the hydrolipid barrier and helps soothe itching. As supportive care for atopic dermatitis, allergies, dry skin, epidermal flaking and skin hypersensitivity.

**Ophiopogon japonicus** – soothes skin irritation and reduces sensitivity. It helps reduce transepidermal water loss, thereby restoring skin elasticity. It supports the regeneration of damaged skin by accelerating epidermal renewal. It strengthens the skin’s resistance to external factors and reduces flaking. It helps restore the balance of the skin’s microbiota. It improves the appearance and softness of the coat.

**Ectoin** is a natural compound with moisturising and protective properties. By binding water, it forms a protective layer on the skin surface, helping to prevent transepidermal water loss and subsequent dryness. It helps soothe itching associated with irritation, skin allergies and atopic dermatitis. It supports the repair of damaged epidermis and helps maintain the balance of the skin’s microbiota.

###   USE

 Before bathing, brush the coat thoroughly. Wet the entire body with lukewarm water, apply the shampoo and distribute evenly. Massage until a lather forms, then rinse. Repeat the application, leaving the shampoo on the coat and skin for a few minutes. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Use 2–3 times per week or as needed.

###   INDICATIONS

 As supportive care for atopic dermatitis, allergies, dry skin, epidermal flaking and skin hypersensitivity.
