# effective

## purpose

Witamins for dogs and cats is used to improve the quality of coat. It is particularly recommended during shedding periods. It also helps to compensate for nutritional deficiencies and satisfies the needs of convalescing animals. An addition of hydrolysed liver extract provides B vitamins and, especially, haematopoietic agents. Due to the content of amino acids, such as isoleucine, leucine, methionine, phenylalanine, threonine, valine, positively affects functioning of the body. Vitamins influence immunological and metabolic processes of the body.

###   Use

 Mix with food:

-

**dogs and cats** < 5 kg of body weight – 5 drops/day

-

**dogs and cats** 5-15 kg of body weight – 10 drops/day

-

**dogs** 15-30 kg of body weight  – 15 drops/day

-

**dogs** 30-45 kg of body weight – 20 drops/day

-

**dogs** > 45kg of body weight – 25 drops/day

1 ml contains 20 drops.

The product contains protein of animal origin. It can be fed to animals other than ruminants.

###   Indications

 Skin and coat regenerator. Shine fur. Healthy skin.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Ingredients

 Composition: Propylene glycol, products of animal origin. Analytical elements: Humidity 87.4%, crude oils and fats 0.9%, crude protein 0.3%, crude ash 0.1%, crude fibre 0%. 1-litre additives: Vitamin A 2g, biotin 0,5 g, vitamin B6 4 g, nicotine acid 8 g, calcium pantothenate 8 g, zinc gluconate 0,3 g.
