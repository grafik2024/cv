## purpose

The shampoo is formulated for both washing of the coat and for easy rinsing.  This facilitates combing. The coat becomes soft and shiny. Plant ingredients reduce hair loss and dandruff formation. Chamomile extract strengthens the natural colour of the hair.

###   Use

 For external use only. Shake well before use. Wet the coat, apply the shampoo, lather, wash and rinse. Repeat the action, leaving the shampoo on the fur and skin for a few minutes. Rinse with lukewarm water, dry and brush. After washing use the hydrating fluid HYDRA-DERM N.

###   Indications

 Long hair care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions
