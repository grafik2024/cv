## purpose

**WITA-VET junior+adult drops** is intended for puppies, bitches and pregnant as well as nursing cats, convalescing dogs, dogs intended for breeding and show dogs as well as animals subject to prolonged effort. The product is also used for poor appetite and with older animals. The ingredients contained in the food have a beneficial influence on the general metabolism of the body and bone mineralization, they facilitate proper functioning of sebaceous glands and help to keep the skin supple and the coat shiny.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food:

-

**dogs and cats** < 5 kg of body weight – 5 drops / day

-

**dogs** 5-15 kg of body weight – 10 drops / day

-

**dogs** 15-30 kg of body weight – 15 drops / day

-

**dogs** 30-45 kg of body weight – 20 drops / day

-

**dogs** > 45kg of body weight – 25 drops / day

 Growth. Pregnancy. Lactation. Vitality.
