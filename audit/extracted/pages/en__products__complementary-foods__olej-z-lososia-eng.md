## PROPERTIES

Salmon oil is a source of omega-3 fatty acids (EPA and DHA), which have a beneficial effect on many metabolic processes in the body of dogs and cats. EPA has an anti-inflammatory effect, while DHA contributes to the development of brain structures and the retina of the eye. Salmon oil influences the balance between omega-3 and omega-6 fatty acids and significantly lowers blood cholesterol levels. Regular use of the product makes your dog’s or cat’s coat thick, springy and glossy. Salmon oil is recommended for brittle and dull coat, excessive hair loss, dandruff and dry skin. It has a soothing effect on skin irritations, allergies and eczema. It supports the body in cases of immune system disorders and improves reproductive capacity.

###   USE

 Instructions for use: Dogs and cats: less than 5 kg b.w. : 2,5 ml per day from 5 up to 15kg b.w. : 5 ml per day from 15 up to 30 kg b.w. : 10 ml per day over 30 kg b.w. : 15 ml per day

###   INDICATIONS

 Salmon oil use is recommeneded in conditions such as: brittle and dull coat, hair loss, dandruff, dry skin, skin inflammation, itching, allergies, eczemas, pigmentation issues, immune system deficiencies, reproductive issues.
