## purpose

Emulsion helps to remove excess earwax from internal ear canal and from earlobe. Softens and helps eliminate calluses.

###   Use

 Apply 5 - 30 drops (depending on the size of the animal) of the lotion to the ear canal. Spread by rubbing the base of the ear and then remove the excess with a swab. Clean the inside of the earlobe at the same time. Repeat until the ear is fully cleaned. Use once or twice a week, depending on the amount of earwax produced.
