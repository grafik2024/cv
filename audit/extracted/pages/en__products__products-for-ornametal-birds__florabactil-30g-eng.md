## purpose

**FLORABACTIL** is intended for ornamental birds. Proper digestive processes are necessary to use all nutrients well. The feed ingredients positively affect digestion, enhance birds’ weakened ability to digest proteins, which prevents the loss of body weight and emaciation.

###   Use

 2 measuring cups / 30 ml of water (1 teaspoon) / 300 ml of water). Administer for 3 consecutive days in a week for a few months.

**PARROTS**: 4 measuring cups / 5 ml of water (1 teaspoon) and soak 30 g of grains or 2 teaspoons / 45 ml of water and soak 300 g of grains.

Administer for 3 consecutive days in a week for a few months. 1 measuring cup contains 0,16 of the feed.

###   INDICATIONS

 Digestion.

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For ornamental birds.

###   Ingredients

 Composition: crude protein 2,48%, crude fibre 0,1%,crude ash 0,4%, crude oils and fats 0,5%, lysine 0,18%, methionine 0,03%, calcium 0,01%, phosphorus 0%, sodium 0%, humidity 5%
