## purpose

**KARBOWITAL** positively boosts functioning of the digestive system with gastrointestinal disorders. Colloidal carbon and acacia gum protect digestive tract mucosa against irritants, absorb harmful substances and help to restore proper functioning of intestines. The product is a tasty treat, thus willingly consumed by animals.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food: 1 tab. per 10 kg b.w., 2-3 times a day or follow veterinarian recommendations.

###   Indications

 Gastrointestinal protection.

 For dogs and cats.
