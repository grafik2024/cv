Complementary feed – mineral recommended for daily use for dogs of all breeds, puppies and adult dogs fed ready-made or home-made food. Reduces shedding. Recommended for dry, dull and damaged coats. The ingredients in the feed help restore skin suppleness, shine, colour and resilience of the coat. They regulate the sebaceous glands and help to keep the skin moisturised. When preparing dogs for dog shows, start application 3 weeks before the show.
