## purpose

**FLORATRANSIT** supports functioning of the digestive system, helps to maintain the proper consistency of droppings and provides electrolytes. The product has a protective effect on the digestive system and stimulates the development of good intestinal flora. It also limits the loss of water through the intestine wall.

###   Use

 1 measuring cup / 40 g of mashed food 1 measuring cup contains ca 0,12 g of the feed.

###   INDICATIONS

 Digestive tract. Intestinal flora.

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For ornamental birds.

###   Ingredients

 Composition: magnesium oxide, sodium chloride Analytical constituents: magnesium 2,1%, sodium 1,5%, lysine 0%, methionine 0%, calcium 0 %, phosphorus 0% Additives in 1 kg: binding and anti-caking agents: E558 bentonite-montmorillonite 955 g
