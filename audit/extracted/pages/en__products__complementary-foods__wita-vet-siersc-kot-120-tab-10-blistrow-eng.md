## purpose

Complementary feed for cats for use during the shedding period, for dry and damaged fur and when preparing animals for shows. The ingredients restore skin elasticity, giving shine, colour and resilience to the coat. They regulate the sebaceous glands and help to keep the skin hydrated. Exceptionally tasty product, easily accepted by animals.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food:

Cats up to 4 kg – 1 tab. / once a day

Cats over 4 kg – 2 tabs. / once a day

 

When preparing cats for shows, start 3 weeks before the planned show. Use until the coat and skin condition is perfect. Administer directly or mix with the feed.

###   Indications

 REDUCES SHEDDING | SHINY COAT | HEALTHY SKIN

###   for which animals

 For cats.
