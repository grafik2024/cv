## purpose

Stoma-gel toothpaste for dogs and cats with beef scent. The added fragrance makes the animals readily accept the application of the paste. Chlorhexidine contained in the paste has an inhibitory effect on the development of undesirable microflora in the oral cavity, removes unpleasant odor and maintains hygiene of teeth and gums. Chlorhexidine supports the treatment of inflammation of the gums. Inhibits plaque buildup. protects against bacteria for 8-12 hours. Cleaning agents prevent tartar buildup and produce an emulsion that, with food particles, helps remove debris from the surface of teeth and gums.

The addition of a balanced beef aroma makes paste application a positive experience for pets. The scent not only facilitates acceptance of the paste, but also creates a pleasant experience for the animal during daily grooming. Animals eagerly submit to the treatment, which translates into regular use of Stoma-Gel paste and maintenance of a healthy mouth.

Toothbrush for dogs and cats inside the package.

###   Use

 1-2 time(s) a week or every day, depending on the condition of teeth. Apply the paste using a toothbrush, finger or gauze. Rub in gently. Do not rinse.

###   Indications

 Oral hygiene.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.
