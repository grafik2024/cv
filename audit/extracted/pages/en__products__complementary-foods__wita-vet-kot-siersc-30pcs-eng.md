# effective

## purpose

Complementary feed for cats for use during the shedding period, for dry and damaged fur and when preparing animals for shows. The ingredients restore skin elasticity, giving shine, colour and resilience to the coat. They regulate the sebaceous glands and help to keep the skin moist. Exceptionally tasty product, easily accepted by animals.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food:

Cats up to 4 kg – 1 tab. / 1 time per day.

Cats over 4 kg – 2 tabs. / 1 time per day.

When preparing cats for shows, start 3 weeks before the planned show. Use until coat and skin condition is perfect. Administer directly or mix with the feed.

###   Indications

 REDUCES SHEDDING | SHINY COAT | HEALTHY SKIN

###   for which animals

 For cats.

###   Ingredients

 Ingredients: Brewer’s yeast – dead (from Saccharomyces cerevisiae), processed animal protein (beef liver), crude lecithin. Amino acids: 3c301 DL-methionine 50000 mg. Trace elements: 3b605 zinc sulfate monohydrate 27600 mg, 3b202 calcium iodate 308 mg, 3b405 copper II sulfate pentahydrate 2750 mg.
