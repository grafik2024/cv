## purpose

The foam shampoo is designed specifically for dogs that don’t like baths. Since it’s a foam it may be used while travelling or before dog shows, where water may not be available. Its ingredients allow for thorough cleaning of coat and skin. It makes the coat soft, shiny and easy to brush.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Apply foam to a damp washing glove and rub gently into the coat. Wash section by section, regularly adding the shampoo, until the entire body is cleaned. Wipe off with a towel and brush.

###   Indications

 Coat and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 Aqua, Sodium Laureth Sulfate, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, Glicerin, Sodium Chloride, Polysorbate 20, Perfum, DMDM Hydantoin, Methylchloroisothiazolinone, Methylisothiazolinone, Citric Acids
