## purpose

**FLORACAL** is intended for ornamental birds as a food supplement, it ensures adequate supply of basic minerals and amino-acids. The product contains easily assimilated micro- and macro-elements necessary for the proper growth, development and functioning of the body. The feed contains ingredients which influence the proper formation of egg shell and is a necessary food supplement for birds during the breeding season.

###   Use

 5 drops of the feed / 60 ml of water (1 teaspoon / 1 l of water): - once a week; - once a week during the growth of the young, preparation to breeding and 15 days after moulting has finished PARROTS: 5 drops of the feed / 5 ml of water (a teaspoon) and soak 30 g of grains or 2 teaspoons of the feed / 150 ml of water and soak 1 kg of grains: - once a week; - once a day during the growth of the young, preparation to breeding and 15 days after moulting has finished 1 ml contains 20 drops.

###   INDICATIONS

 Growth. Development. Reproduction.

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For ornamental birds.

###   Ingredients

 Composition: calcium chloride, maltodextrin Analytical constituents: crude protein 0%, crude ash 1,4%, crude oils and fats 0,1%,crude fibre 0%, calcium 0,35%, phosphorus 0,4%, sodium 0%, lysine 0,002%, methionine 0%, humidity 86,5% Additives in 1 l: vitamins and provitamins: choline (choline chloride) 140 mg, amino acids and their salts: 3.2.3. L-lysine HCl 20 mg, a mixture of trace elements: E1 iron (iron chloride hexahydrate) 140 mg, E5 manganese (manganese sulphate tetrahydrate) 127 mg, E6 zinc (zinc sulphate heptahydrate) 10 mg, E4 copper (copper chloride dihydrate) 7 mg, preservatives: E338 orthophosphoric acid 17,2 g
