# INFORMATION

## THE DATA WE COLLECT ABOUT YOU

PRIVACY POLICY.

### Data collected during the registration.

To use some of the functions of our system, you need to register. During the registration you will be asked about your e-mail address and the password you will be using on our website.

### Data collected during the purchase transaction.

Before we can finalise a purchase transaction in our shop, we will ask about your name, surname, address and phone number.

## Data collected automatically.

During your visit on our website certain data are collected automatically, e.g. your IP address, domain name, type of browser, type of operating system etc.

### Data collected while contacting us.

When you contact us via the website, telephone, e-mail etc., you pass your personal data to us, e.g. your name, surname, e-mail address etc.

### How do we use your data?

We will never sell your data to third parties.

The data collected during the registration will be used exclusively to enable you to log into our system. Registration is not mandatory.

The data collected during a purchase transaction will be used to carry out the transaction, e.g. receive your payment and send the goods to the provided address. Therefore, your data may be shared with a company processing payment transactions to charge your credit card. The company will not use the data for any other purpose.

If you subscribed for our Newsletter, or otherwise agreed to receive it, we will use your data to send it to you. You will be asked if you want to receive our Newsletter at the registration.

The data collected automatically may be used to analyse user behaviour on our website, to gather demographic data about our users or to personalise the content of our websites. The data of each user is collected automatically.

The data collected in the course of correspondence between you and our Service will be used only to respond to your queries.

In the event of inspection conducted by the Inspector General for Personal Data Protection, your personal data can be made available to the inspectors, in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act.

In the event of infringement of our Service regulations, violation of the law, or if legal provisions so require, we may provide your data to the judicial authorities.

### How will we contact you?

If you purchased goods, we may send you e-mails related to the transaction. We may also contact you on the phone, should we have important information regarding the transaction.

If you subscribed for our Newsletter, or otherwise agreed to receive it, you will receive the Newsletter by e-mail.

### How can you inform us about changes in your data?

After logging into the system and selecting My account, you may introduce any changes in your personal data collected during the registration, and used for logging into the system or sending our Newsletter.

You have got the right to demand that we stop using any of your data or remove it from our database. Such request must be sent in writing to the personal data administrator.

Service: the Internet service available at www.eurowet.pl.

Cookies: IT data, especially text files, stored on the terminal device of the Service User, used to operate the Service websites. Cookies usually contain the name of the website they come from, duration of storage on the terminal device, and a unique number.

The administrator informs that the Service collects exclusively the information contained in cookie files, and no other data about the User. In particular, the Administrator declares that the Service does not use tracking cookies, i.e. files which enable spying on Users.

To adjust the Service content to User’s preferences, and to optimise the website’s user experience. In particular, these files allow to recognise the User’s device and display the website adjusted to the User’s individual needs.

To maintain the User’s session (after logging in), so that the User does not have to log in on each Service subpage.

To create statistics for the analysis of the Internet User behaviour.

### The Administrator informs that two types of cookies are used by the Service:

Session cookies, temporary files that are stored on the User’s terminal device until logging out, leaving the website or closing the Internet browser.

Persistent cookies, i.e. files that are stored on the User’s terminal device for the time determined in the cookie file settings, or until they are removed by the User.

Files that enable using services offered by the website, e.g. certifying cookies used for the services that require certification.

Files that ensure security, used e.g. to identify fraud in certification for the Service-related purposes.

Files that enable collecting information about the pattern of using the Service’s website.

Files that allow to store the settings selected by the User, and to personalise the User’s interface, e.g. the language or region of origin. Files that allow to provide Users with advertisements based on their interests (advertisement cookies).
