## purpose

**Sebovet – Clean** is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic formulated for dogs with symptoms of greasy seborrhea. Juniper oil influences sebum secretion and prevents excessive exfoliation.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Wet the coat thoroughly with warm water. Pour the shampoo over the wet coat and skin and distribute evenly over the whole body (avoiding the eyes). Massage well into the coat, working up a good lather. Rinse with warm water. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the hair and skin for a 5-10 minutes before rinsing off. Use according to need. For the final rinsing the use of moisturizing fluid Hydra-Derm N is recommended.

###   Indications

 Greasy coat (greasy seborrhoea), sticky dandruff flakes, red, irritated skin patches.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 Aqua, Sodium Laureth Sulfate, Glicerin, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, PEG 4 Rapeseedamide, Sodium Chloride, Polisorbate 20, Junipures Oxycedrus Wood Oil, DMDM Hydantoin, Methylchloroisothiazolinone, Methylisothiazolinone, Citric Acid
