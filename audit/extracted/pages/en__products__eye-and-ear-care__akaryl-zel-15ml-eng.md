## purpose

**AKARYL –gel** soothes irritation of external ear canal. Promotes natural regeneration of epidermis and helps to eliminate parasites.

 

###   Use

 Apply a few drops into the external ear canal and ear flap. Gently rub the base of the ear. Remove excess emulsion and wash the ear again. Apply once a day for 7 days. After a week-long break, use again for the next 7 days

###   Indications

 Hygiene of external ear canal and ear flap (pinna).

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Discontinue use in case of intolerance.
