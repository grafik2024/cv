## purpose

**DIARSED **The feed helps maintain the proper functioning of the digestive system and supports it in gastrointestinal disorders.  1 tab. / 10 kg body weight / twice a day. Apply for 3–5 days. Contains animal protein, for feeding animals other than ruminants.
