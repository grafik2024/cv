## purpose

Stoma-Gel is a toothpaste for cats and dogs, with a beef flavour. The fragrance additive makes it easier for animals to accept the application of the paste. The chlorhexidine in the paste inhibits the growth of unwanted microflora in the mouth, removes unpleasant odours, and maintains the hygiene of the teeth and gums. Chlorhexidine supports the treatment of gum inflammation. It inhibits the formation of dental plaque and protects against bacteria for 8-12 hours. The cleaning agents prevent the formation of tartar and create an emulsion that, with food particles, helps remove impurities from the surface of the teeth and gums.

The addition of a balanced beef flavour makes the application of the paste a positive experience for animals. The fragrance not only makes it easier for the animal to accept the paste but also creates a pleasant experience during daily care. Animals willingly undergo the procedure, which results in regular use of Stoma-Gel paste and helps maintain a healthy mouth.

###   Use

 1-2 time(s) a week or every day, depending on the condition of teeth. Apply the paste using a toothbrush, finger or gauze. Rub in gently. Do not rinse.

###   Indications

 Oral hygiene.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.
