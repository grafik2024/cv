## purpose

The shampoo contains surfactants which offer thorough cleansing and easy rinsing. It leaves coat and skin thoroughly cleaned, supple,  shiny and moisturized. Additional ingredients make brushing easier after washing. The coat becomes soft and shiny.

###   Use

 For external use only. Shake well before use. Wet the coat, apply the shampoo, spread, lather and rinse. Repeat the action, leaving the shampoo on the fur and skin for a few minutes. Rinse with lukewarm water, dry and brush.

###   Indications

 Coat and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.
