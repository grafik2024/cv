## purpose

**PEROXYVET** is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic for oily skin and coat. It promotes healthy skin microflora and regulates and functioning of the sebaceous glands. It relieves skin irritation and facilitates natural skin regeneration. The addition of gentle washing substances helps to keep skin and coat in good condition.

###   Use

 Wet the coat thoroughly with warm water. Pour the shampoo over the wet coat and skin and distribute evenly over the whole body (avoiding the eyes). Massage well into the coat, working up a good lather. Rinse with warm water. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the hair and skin for a 5-10 minutes before rinsing off. Use according to need. For the final rinsing the use of moisturizing fluid Hydra-Derm N is recommended.

###   Indications

 Oily skin and coat care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.
