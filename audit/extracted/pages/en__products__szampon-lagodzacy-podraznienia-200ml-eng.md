# effective

## purpose

The shampoo has very good cleansing properties and is easy to rinse off. The juniper oil soothes irritated skin and reduces flaking.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Wet the coat and skin thoroughly with warm water, pour on the shampoo, spread evenly (avoiding the eyes), massage in and rinse off. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the coat and skin for 5-10 minutes. Rinse off with lukewarm water, dry and brush.

###   Indications

 Coat and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 Juniper oil, delicate washing base, moisturising agent
