## purpose

**FLORACAL** is intended for ornamental birds as a food supplement necessary during the breeding period. It has a beneficial influence on breeding capacity of male and female pigeons as well as hatch rate.

###   Use

 8 drops of the feed / 60 ml of water (1 teaspoon / 1 l of water)- twice a week for 3 weeks before breeding and throughout the whole breeding period

**PARROTS**: 8 drops of the feed / 5 ml of water (a teaspoon) and soak 30 g of grains or 1 teaspoon / 75 ml of water and soak 500 g of grains- twice a week for 3 weeks before breeding and throughout the whole breeding period 1 ml contains 20 drops.
