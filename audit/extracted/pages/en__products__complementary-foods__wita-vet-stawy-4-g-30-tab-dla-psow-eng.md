## purpose

Complementary feed supports the proper functioning of the joints. Recommended for dogs with mobility problems. Can be given to young dogs during the period of intensive growth. The ingredients contained in the product – chondroitin, glucosamine, devil’s claw, fish collagen, methylsulfonylmethane (MSM) and hyaluronic acid – strengthen the function of the joints and support their mobility and flexibility. Rosehip extract provides vitamin C, which is involved in the synthesis of joint cartilage collagen.

###   Use
