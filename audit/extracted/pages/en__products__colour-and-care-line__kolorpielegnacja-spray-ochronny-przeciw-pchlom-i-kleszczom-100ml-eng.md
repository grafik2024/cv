## purpose

The preparation helps to protect dogs and cats from fleas, ticks and other insects.

###   Use

 Brush the coat against its natural direction. Spray evenly from a distance of 20 - 30 cm while spreading over the coat (avoiding the eyes). Rub the preparation into the coat and skin. Apply once a week according to need.

###   Indications

 For coat and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with animal's eyes. If the product accidently gets into the eyes, rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Do not use on dogs and cats below 3 months of age, or on pregnant or nursing animals. To minimize the risk of the animal getting fleas or ticks, change its bed periodically.

###   Ingredients
