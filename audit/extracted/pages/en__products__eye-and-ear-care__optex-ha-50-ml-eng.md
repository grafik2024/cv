## purpose

**Optex with hyaluronic acid** is a veterinary dermocosmetic, which alleviates symptoms associated with the irritation of the conjunctiva and supports the natural moisturising of the eyes and eyelids. It helps the removal of contaminations from the conjunctival sac. The **hyaluronic acid** contained in the product moisturises the cornea, dryness and eye pain, while supporting the regeneration of  ocular surface structures after surgical procedures

###   Use

 Moisten a swab and carefully wet the eyes or apply a few drops of the liquid into the conjunctival sac. Remove its excess. Wipe the eyelids. Use when necessary, even every day.

###   Indications

 Eye and eyelid hygiene.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Discontinue use in case of intolerance.

###   Ingredients

 Aqua, Centaurea Cyanus Floral Water, Sodium Chloride, Benzalkonium Chloride, Hydrolyzed Hyaluronic Acid, Sodium Methylparaben, Ortofosforic Acid, CI 42090
