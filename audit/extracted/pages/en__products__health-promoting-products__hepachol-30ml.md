## purpose

**HEPACHOL** syrup positively affects functioning of the digestive system, helps in functioning of the liver. The food ingredients stimulate intestinal peristalsis and bile production as well as secretion of digestive enzymes. The product is recommended in case of overfeeding, decreased appetite, weakness, constipation, vomiting.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food:

**dogs and cats**: body weight up to 7kg : 5ml/day;

-

body weight 7-12 kg: 10 ml / day

-

body weight 12-18 kg: 15 ml / day

-

body weight 18-25 kg: 20 ml / day

-

body weight > 25 kg: 30 ml / day.

Twice a day for 8-15 days.
**Pigeons**: Administer with drinking water: – 5 ml / 1 litre of water for 7 – 10 days – during the flight period, use the same amount once a week.

###   Indications

 Supports the work of the digestive system and liver.

###   for which animals

 For dogs, cats, pigeons.

###   Ingredients

 Ingredients: sorbitol, propylene glycol, plant processing products: artichoke (Cynara scolymus), boldo leaf (Pneumus boldus), rosemary (Rosmarinus officinalis), dandelion (Taraxacum officinale). Additives in 1 kg: nutritional: amino acids: L-lysine HCl 15.7g. Analytical constituents: crude protein 2.4%, crude fiber 0%, crude fat 0%. crude ash 0.3%.
