## purpose

**EFADERM-E** improves skin condition in skin inflammation or excessive hair loss. Its use helps to keep the skin and coat in good condition.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food: - convalescence: 1 ml / 5 kg of body weight / day for 3-4 weeks; - seborrhea, pruritus: 2 ml / 5 kg of body weight / day for 3-4 weeks; Recommended period of use: up to 2 months. It is advisable to consult a veterinarian before use.

###   Indications

 Coat. Skin.
