## purpose

**EFADERM-E** supports skin functions in case of skin inflammation or excessive hair loss. It makes it easier to keep the skin and coat in good condition.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food:

-

**convalescence**: 1 ml / 5 kg of body weight / day for 3-4 weeks

-

**seborrhea, pruritus**: 2 ml / 5 kg of body weight / day for 3-4 weeks

###   Indications

 Coat. Skin.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Ingredients

 Composition: fish oil, plant oils and fats (borage oil- Borago officinalis and maize-- germ oil- Zea mays). Analytical constituents: crude oils and fats 99,0%, omega-3 acids in total 22,52% (eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA) 13,5%, docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) 9,0%, alpha-Lino- lenic acid (ALA) 0,02%, omega-6 acids in total 7,4% (linoleic acid (LA) 5,2%, gamma-Li- nolenic acid (GLA) 2,2%), crude protein 0,5%, crude ash 0,2%, humidity 0,05%, crude fibre 0%.
