## purpose

**OPTEX** is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic that soothes conjunctival irritation and helps to retain natural moisture in the eyes and eyelids. It also helps to remove the impurities accumulated in the conjunctival sac.

###   Use

 Moisten a swab and carefully wet the eyes or apply a few drops of the liquid into the conjunctival sac. Remove its excess. Wipe the eyelids. Use when necessary, even every day.

###   Indications

 Eye and eyelid hygiene.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Discontinue use in case of intolerance.

###   Ingredients

 Aqua, Centaurea Cyanus Floral Water, Sodium Chloride, Benzalkonium Chloride, Sodium Methylparaben, Ortofosforic Acid, CI 42090
