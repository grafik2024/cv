## purpose

**OTISEPTOL** is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic containing ingredients that help to remove earwax from the external ear canal ear flap (pinna). They soften rough epidermis and thus make its removal easier and regeneration faster.

###   Use

 Apply 5-30 drops (depending on the size of the pet) of the emulsion into the ear canal. Distribute, gently rubbing the base of the ear and then remove excess liquid with a swab. At the same time, clean the interior of the ear flap. Repeat until the ear is fully cleaned. Depending on the amount of earwax, use once or twice a week.

###   Indications

 Hygiene of external ear canal and ear flap.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients
