## purpose

**AlervetTM excellence** is a veterinary dermocosmetic which provides relief for pruritus and inflamed skin. Phytosphingosine restores the protective barrier of the skin and keeps it moisturized. The shampoo has perfect cleaning properties and makes it easier to remove dead cells and sebum from the surface of the skin.

###   Use

 Carefully brush your dog or cat before the bath. Wet the coat and the skin with warm water, apply the shampoo over the whole body and spread carefully. Massage gently until foam is formed, then rinse it. Repeat the actions. Allow the shampoo to remain on the coat and the skin for few minutes and rinse thoroughly. -frequency of use: use 2-3 times per week or in accordance with the recommendations of the veterinarian.

###   Indications

 Skin and coat care in treatment of inflamed skin. Soothing pruritus

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.
