## purpose

The drops offer extra, reliable protection against fleas and ticks. Safe for you, your loved ones and the environment! Free from allergens and irritation. Effectively offers extra protection against fleas and ticks.

Neem extract is a natural ingredient with antibacterial, antifungal, anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. It also acts as a natural repellent. It helps repel mosquitoes, ticks and other parasites that may be present on the animal’s skin and coat. Its soothing and anti-inflammatory properties help relieve itching caused by bites.

###   Use

 Shake the pipette well before opening. Part the fur and apply the contents of the pipette(s) evenly across the entire surface of the animal’s coat and skin. Use as needed. Cats 4 kg – 2ml, 1 pipette ;Dogs 15 kg – 4ml, 2 pipettes

###   Indications

 This preparation supports the protection of dogs and cats against fleas and ticks on the skin and coat.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with the eyes. Wash hands thoroughly after each use. For external use only.
