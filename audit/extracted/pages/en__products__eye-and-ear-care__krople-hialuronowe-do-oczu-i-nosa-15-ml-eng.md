## purpose

Moisturising drops for the eyes and nose, intended for dogs and cats.  

The drops relieve the symptoms of dry eye syndrome caused by reduced or absent tears. They alleviate the effects of irritation caused by external factors (e.g. wind, sun, dust, etc.). They protect the nasal mucosa from drying out, promote healing, and restore natural moisture. They are designed to protect and moisturise the eyes and nose. They support eye regeneration during pharmacotherapy.

**Hyaluronic acid (eye)** – protects the eye from irritation and drying out, and is an important factor in long-lasting moisturisation of the eye surface. Thanks to its hyaluronic acid content, the product supports the formation of the tear film, stabilises the corneal environment, and supports its regeneration.

 Use in cases of mechanical irritation (e.g. burning, itching, tearing) caused by external factors such as wind, sun, pharmacotherapy, and in daily care.
