## purpose

**Hexoderm** is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic. It regulates the skin’s microflora by stabilising its pH and prevents drying by the creation of a thin fat-protein layer. The addition of gentle washing substances allow for frequent use.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Wet the coat thoroughly with lukewarm water. Pour the shampoo over the wet coat and skin and distribute evenly over the whole body (avoiding the eyes). Massage well into the coat, working up a good lather. Rinse with lukewarm water. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the hair and skin for a 5-10 minutes before rinsing off. Use according to need. For the final rinsing the hydrating fluid Hydra-Derm N is recommended.

###   Indications

 Skin care during treatment of inflammation.

###   for which animals

 For rodents and fur animals

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.
