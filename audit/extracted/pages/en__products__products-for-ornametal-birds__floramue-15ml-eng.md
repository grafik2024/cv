## purpose

**FLORAMUE** is intended for ornamental birds as a food supplement. It ensures adequate supply of vitamins, minerals and amino-acids necessary for the proper formation of feathers and functioning of the body. The feed contains methionine, which positively affects the moulting process.

###   Use

 2 drops of the feed / 60 ml of water (or 35 drops / 1 l of water). Administer throughout the whole moulting period.

**PARROTS**: 2 drops of the feed / 5 ml of water (1 teaspoon) and soak 30 g of grains or 35 drops / 75 ml of water and soak 500 g of grains. Administer throughout the whole moulting period. 1 ml contains 20 drops.

###   INDICATIONS

 Color of feathers.

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For ornamental birds.
