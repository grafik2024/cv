## purpose

The shampoo is formulated for both washing of the coat and for easy rinsing, facilitating combing. The fur becomes soft and shiny. Plant ingredients soften and elasticize the epidermis. The henna extract enhances the natural colour of the hair.

###   Use

 For external use only. Shake well before use. Wet the coat, apply the shampoo, lather, wash and rinse. Repeat the action, leaving the shampoo on the fur and skin for a few minutes. Rinse with lukewarm water, dry and brush. After washing use the hydrating fluid HYDRA-DERM N.

###   Indications

 For dark coats and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.
