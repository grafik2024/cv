## properties

**HEXODERM-K** is a veterinary dermocosmetic which regulates microflora of the skin by stabilising pH and prevents drying by the formation of a protective fat-protein layer. Chlorhexidine and ketoconazole kill harmful microorganisms that cause skin conditions. Addition of gentle washing substances allow for frequent use.

###   USE

 Shake well before use. Wet the coat thoroughly with warm water. Pour the shampoo over the wet coat and skin and distribute evenly over the whole body (avoiding the eyes). Massage well into the coat, working up a good lather. Rinse off with warm water. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the hair and skin for a 5-10 minutes before rinsing off. Use according to needs. For the final rinse, the use of moisturizing fluid Hydra-Derm N is recommended.

###   INDICATIONS

 Use on dry skin or on skin prone to seborrhea and/or allergic reactions.

###   PRECAUTIONS

 Avoid contact with eyes.
