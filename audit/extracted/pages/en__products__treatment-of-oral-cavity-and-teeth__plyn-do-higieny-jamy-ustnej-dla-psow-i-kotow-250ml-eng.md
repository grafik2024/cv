## purpose

Complementary feed for dogs and cats for maintaining good oral and dental hygiene. Natural rockrose and peppermint extracts support the healthy bacterial microflora of the oral cavity and thus ensure fresh breath.

###   Use

 Shake the bottle before use. Dissolve 5 ml of liquid in 250 ml of your pet’s drinking water daily. Change the water in the bowl daily.
