# effective

## purpose

**FLORAMOLE** improves functioning of the respiratory and digestive systems by stimulating birds’ immune system.

###   Use

 3 drops of the feed/ 60 ml of water (2 ml / 1 l of water). Administer once a month for the period of 5-10 days.

**PARROTS**: 3 drops of the feed / 5 ml of water (1 teaspoon) and soak 30 g of grains or 2 ml / 75 of water and soak 500 g of grains. Administer once a month for the period of 5-10 days. 1 ml contains 20 drops. Net quantity: 15 ml

###   INDICATIONS

 Respiratory, digestive, immune system.

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For ornamental birds.

###   Ingredients

 Composition: sugar syrup, eucalyptus extract (Eucalyptus), peppermint extract (Mentha piperita), thyme extract (Thymus vulgaris), pine extract (Pinus silvestris) Analytical constituents: crude protein 1,3%, crude ash 0,3%, crude oils and fats 2,3%,crude fibre 1,3%, lysine 0%, methionine 0%, calcium 0%, phosphorus 0%, sodium 0,2%, humidity 46% Additives in 1 l: emulsifying and stabilizing agents: E484 glyceryl polyethyleneglycol ricinoleate 38 500 mg
