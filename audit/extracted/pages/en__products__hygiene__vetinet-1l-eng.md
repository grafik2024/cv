# effective

## purpose

No irritation of skin, mucous membranes and wounds. Effectively removes biological and mechanical contaminants. It has very good cleaning properties. Recommended for surfaces and general use.

###   Use

 Apply a small amount of liquid to the area to be washed and spread. Wash and rinse with water. Repeat the activity.

###   Indications

 Washing before and after contact with an animal, washing animals before a surgery, washing before contact with food. Hygiene of the veterinary office and equipment.

###   for whom

 General purpose liquid.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 AQUA, SODIUM LAURETH SULFATE, COCAMIDOPROPYL BETAINE, CHLORHEXIDINE DIGLUCONATE, PEG 4 RAPESEEDAMIDE, GLICERIN, SILICONE QUATERIUM-17, LAURETH-9, POLYSORBATE 20, EUCALYPTUS RADIATA ESSENTIAL OIL, CITRIC ACID, DMDM HYDANTOIN, METHYLCHLOROISOTHIAZOLINONE, METHYLISOTHIAZOLINONE, CI42090
