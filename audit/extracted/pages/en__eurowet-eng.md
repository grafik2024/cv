# About us

## Veterinary and hygienic products manufacturer

Manufacturer and distributor of products for dogs, cats and other domestic pets. We offer over 100 specialist products. All products are based on natural ingredients and produced using innovative technologies.

We cooperate with largest  product distributors in Poland.

     Veterinary dermocosmetics

A group of preparations facilitating the maintenance of proper condition of skin and hair. Used in prevention and supportive treatment of dermatological diseases. Our set is supplemented with preparations accelerating wound healing and moisturizing skin and hair. Designed for dogs, cats and other companion animals.

     Health-promoting products for animals

Facilitating the maintenance of good animal health status. Used in disorders of the locomotion & digestive system, liver, pancreas and kidneys. Contains plant extracts. Safe for animals and the environment. Our offer include complementary food and feed as well as probiotics.

     Complementary foods

Providing the right amount of vitamins and minerals. Keeping animals in good health throughout their lives. Regular administration is essential during pregnancy, lactation and convalescence. Tasty and aromatic. Easy to administer.

     Eye and ear care products

They moisturise and reduce irritations, as well as remove impurities.

     Products for oral and dental care

A line of products for oral and dental hygiene, preventing tartar and unpleasant odours.

     Colour&Care cosmetic line, Amiwet

Modern products developed using the technologies of leading French veterinary companies.

     Products for birds

They provide essential vitamins, mineral compounds and amino acids.

     Hygienic products

Effective ingredients. Handy, conventient packaging. Precise dispensing. Pleasant scent.
