## purpose

**Tanaderm** is a veterinary nourishing dermocosmetic soothing damaged foot pads and stimulating renewal of the epidermis. Its ingredients regulate the skin microflora and demonstrate astringent effect.

###   Use

 After cleaning damaged foot pads spray or spray onto a cosmetic pad and rub the damaged area. Dry. Repeat the treatment twice a day. In case of dogs with sensitive pads use depending on the need - 2-3 times a week. Use also in order to strengthen pads of working dogs and dogs at risk of foot pads damage e.g. during hunting, vacations (hard and sharp surfaces, hot asphalt) and in winter time (protection against harmful effects of salt, snow...) - once a day. Do not allow licking off the product.

###   Indications

 Renewal of damaged foot pads and soothing of irritation.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 Aqua, Glicerin, Trichloroacetic acid, Propylene Glycol, Centella Asiatica Extract, Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice, DMDM Hydantoin, Methylchloroisothiazolinone, Methylisothiazolinone, Citric Acid
