## purpose

**OTOLAN** Ear wax removal liquid for dogs and cats. Thanks to specially selected ingredients, thoroghly removes ear wax and impurities that accumulate in the ear canal, thus prevents bacterial growth in ear flap and ear canal.

**Jojoba oil**

Moisturises and protects ear flap and ear canal from adverse external factors.

**Sweet almond oil**

Acts as a natural emolient thanks to unsaturated fatty acid content. Aids ear wax removal.

**Squalane **

Soothes irritation and inflammation. It facilitates natural restoration of ear canal and ear flap lining, as well as ear wax removal.

**Peach kerneloil**

Rich in omega-6 and omega-9 acids. Moisturises and soothes ear flap and ear canal. Vitamin E Aids ear flap and ear canal lining regeneration.

###   Use

 Apply 5 to 30 drops (according to the size of the pet) into the ear canal, massage gently, then cleanse the ear and remove the remaining liquid with cotton pad.

###   Indications

 Hygiene of external ear canal and ear flap.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Keep away from eyes. Do not use cotton buds.

###   Ingredients

 Paraffinum liquidum, Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Oil, Squalane, Simmondsia Chinensis (Jojoba) Seed Oil, Prunus Persica (Peach) Kernel Oil, Tocopherol Acetate.
