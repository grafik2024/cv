## purpose

The shampoo has very good cleansing properties and is easy to rinse. The apricot seed oil in the shampoo makes brushing easier leaving the coat soft and shiny.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Wet the coat and skin thoroughly with warm water, pour on the shampoo, spread evenly(avoiding the eyes), massage in well and rinse. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the coat and skin for several minutes. Wash off with lukewarm water, dry and brush.

###   Indications

 Skin and coat care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 Apricot seed oil, delicate washing base, moisturiser, natural fragrances
