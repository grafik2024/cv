## purpose

Complementary feed that improves: skin function, keratinization process, development and functioning of sebaceous glands and coat quality. The product is a tasty treat thus animals consume it willingly. Recommendations: Administer in increased hair loss, brittle, matt and dry coat. Protects the liver from increased accumulation of copper.

###   USE

 Instructions for use: Administer at least 1 hour before feeding. -skin and coat issues: dogs and cats - ½ piece per 10 kg b.w. – 2 times a day for 4 weeks - liver protection: dogs and cats – 1 piece per 10 kg b.w. – 2 times a day for 4 weeks Caution: in prolonged periods of use consult a veterinarian. Can be used in dogs and cats with cardiac and renal insufficiency.

###   Indications

 Skin and coat regenerator.
