## purpose

Veterinarian dermocosmetic, nourishing soothing damaged digital pads and stimulating renewal of the epidermis. Its ingredients regulate the skin microflora and demonstrate and tightening effect.

###   Use

 After cleaning of damaged digital pads spray or swab them with dampened gauze. Dry. Repeat the treatment 2 times a day. In case of dogs with sensitive digital pads use depending on the needs - 2-3 times a week. Use also in order to strengthen digital pads of working dogs and dogs at risk of digital pads damage e.g. during hunting, vacations (hard and sharp surfaces, hot asphalt) and in winter time (protection against harmful effects of salt, snow...) - once a day for 15 days. Prevent against licking.

###   Indications

 Renewal of damaged digital pads and irritation soothing.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.
