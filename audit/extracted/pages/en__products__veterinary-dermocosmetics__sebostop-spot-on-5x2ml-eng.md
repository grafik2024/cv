## purpose

**Sebostop spot-on** is a veterinary dermocosmetic specially developed for dogs with symptoms of seborrhea. Phytosphingosine aids the reconstruction of the corneal layer of the epidermis, thus reducing seborrhea, and regulates the epidermal microflora. Other components of the product protect the skin against dehydration and soothe irritation.

###   Use

 As a supportive treatment in conjunction with anti-seborrheic shampoos SEBOVET CLEAN or SEBOVET excellence in case of:

**generalized kerato-seborrheic syndrome**: once a week for four weeks.

After moving aside the fur, apply the contents of the pipette to the skin in 2 or 3 places (in the intercostal and lumbar region):

dogs and cats of body mass <20 kg 1 pipette

- dogs of body mass 20-45 kg 2 pipettes

- dogs of body mass > 45 kg 3 pipettes

**local kerato-seborrheic syndrome**: apply 1 pipette contents to the skin in the area of the change 1 time a week, for 4 weeks.

**unpleasantodours:** apply as in case of the generalized kerato-seborrheic syndrome, once a week.

**excess wax in the external earcanal:** the content of ½ – 1 pipette into each ear 2 times a week, for a period of three weeks.

###   Indications

 Kerato-seborrheic skin conditions, general or local. Excess wax in the external ear canal. Unpleasant odour of the skin.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with the eyes

###   Ingredients

 Ethoxydiglycol, PVP, Polyglyceryl-4 Caprate, Phytosphingosine
