# effective

## purpose

Supplementary food for dogs and cats regulating the body’s water and electrolyte balance. When mixed with water, it forms an isotonic solution.

**Glucose** increases the absorption of macro-elements and water as well as providing easily digestible energy. **Electrolytes** contain salts that dissociate into ions, facilitating the transmission of nerve impulses in the body. **Rosehip fruit extract** supplements any vitamin C deficiency. **BCAA complex (Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine), L-glutamine** provide the amino acids necessary for proper regeneration and muscle development. The addition of **liver powder** improves the taste, which makes animals more willing to accept the preparation.

###   Use

 1 sachet (25 g) per 15 kg b.w. - dissolve the preparation in water or mix with food. Naturally cloudy product.

###   Indications

 • dehydration • digestive disorders • diarrhoea • physical exertion • restoration of body homeostasis

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Ingredients

 Glucose, dried beef liver, potassium chloride, sodium chloride, magnesium lactate, rosehip extract. Additives per 1 kg: Amino acids: 3c381 Isoleucine 25 g, 3c382 L-leucine 50 g, 3c370 Valine 25 g, 3c451 L-glutamine 50 g. Analytical ingredients: Crude Protein 22.3 g, Crude Fibre 0.2 g, crude fat 2.3 g, crude ash 11.1 g, calcium 0.1 g, magnesium 5.5 g, sodium 0.2 g, potassium 0.2 g.
