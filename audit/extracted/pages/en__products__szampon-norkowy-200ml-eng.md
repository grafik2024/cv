# effective

## purpose

Mink oil shampoo offers thorough cleaning and is easy to rinse. It nourishes the skin and rebuilds the hair’s natural protective layer. It enhances and regenerates the coat, making it shiny, soft and silky.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Wet the coat and skin thoroughly with warm water, pour on the shampoo, spread evenly (avoiding the eyes), massage in well and rinse. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the coat and skin for several minutes. Rinse off with lukewarm water and dry.

###   Indications

 Coat and skin care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 Mink oil , delicate washing base, moisturisers , natural fragrance
