## purpose

**FLORARUTIL** intensifies the red colour of feathers.

###   Use

 1 measuring cup / 60 ml of water or with food / 15 birds. Administer at the beginning of moulting and for the next 20 days. When combined with FLORAMUE, serve in a mash or mix with grated carrot. 1 measuring cup contains ca 0,12 g of the feed.
