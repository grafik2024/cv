## purpose

Mink oil shampoo cleanses extremely well and is easy to rinse. It nourishes the skin and rebuilds the hair’s natural protective layer, enhancing and regenerating it, and making it shiny, soft and silky.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Wet the coat with warm water, pour on the shampoo, spread evenly (avoiding the eyes), massage in well and rinse. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the coat and skin for several minutes. Rinse off with lukewarm water, dry and brush.
