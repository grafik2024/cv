## purpose

**STRESNAL** contains plant ingredients that have a calming effect on animals. They reduce anxiety, fear and aggression as well as help to relieve muscle tension. The food has a beneficial influence on dogs and cats during their owner’s absence, competitions, shows, journeys and puppy depression.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food: -dogs and cats:2ml/5kg of body weight/day. Administer 2-3 times a day. In case of planned travel, administer 30-60 minutes before the journey.

###   Indications

 EASES STRESS. CALMS DOWN.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Ingredients

 Ingredients: Glycerin, plant processing products: californica mace (Eschscholtzia californica), hawthorn inflorescence (Crataegus oxyacantha), passionflower herb (Passiflora incarnata), St. John's wort herb (Hypericum perforatum). Additives in 1 kg: Dietary: Amino acids: 3c322 L-lysine HCl 20 g. Technological: Preservatives: 1k301 Sodium benzoate 3 g, 1k202 Potassium sorbate 3 g. Analytical Ingredients: Crude protein 1.14%, crude fiber 0.4%, crude fat 0.11%, crude ash 0.63%, moisture 71.8%.
