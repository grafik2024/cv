## purpose

Drops that relieve conjunctival irritation, reduce nasal discharge and soothe swollen eyelids (palpebral edema) . They help to retain natural moisture in the eyes and eyelids and aid in the removal of dirt accumulated in the conjunctival sac.

###   Use

 Apply 1-2 drops into the conjunctival sac or nose, 4-6 times a day

###   Indications

 Maintaining eye and nose hygiene, removing dirt from the conjunctival sac, soothing irritation of the conjunctiva and nasal mucosa.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Discontinue use in case of intolerance.
