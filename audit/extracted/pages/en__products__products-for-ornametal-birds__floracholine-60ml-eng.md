## purpose

**FLORACHOLINE** has an influence on lipid metabolism in the liver, aids in the secretion of bile acids and provides birds with energy. The product positively affects functions of the digestive system, facilitates elimination of fat from the liver and bile production.

###   Use

 10 drops of the feed / 60 ml of water (1 teaspoon / 500 ml of water)- twice a week.

**PARROTS**: 10 drops of the feed / 5 ml of water (a teaspoon) and soak 30 g of grains or 2 teaspoons of the feed / 75 ml of water and soak 500 g of grains- twice a week 1 ml contains 20 drops.

###   INDICATIONS

 Liver. Energy.

###   FOR WHICH ANIMALS

 For ornamental birds.
