## purpose

**Sebovet – Dry** is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic designed to relieve dandruff in cats and dogs, restoring the healthy balance of the epidermis, increasing elasticity of the skin and relieving irritation.

###   Use

 Shake well before use. Wet the coat thoroughly with warm water. Pour the shampoo over the wet coat and skin and distribute evenly over the whole body (avoiding the eyes). Massage well into the coat, working up a good lather. Rinse with warm water. Repeat, leaving the shampoo on the hair and skin for a 5-10 minutes before rinsing off. Use according to need. For the final rinsing the moisturizing fluid Hydra-Derm N is recommended.

###   Indications

 Dandruff (seborrhoea sicca) characterised by large number of scales. Oily coat with dry and easily damaged hair. The addition of gentle washing substances allow for frequent use.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.

###   Ingredients

 Aqua, Sodium Laureth Sulfate, Cocamidopropyl Betaine, PEG 4 Rapeseedamide, Sodium Chloride, Hydrolyzet Keratin Parfum, Polisorbate 20, DMDM Hydantoin, Methylchloroisothiazolinone, Methylisothiazolinone, Citric Acid
