# effective

## purpose

**Zn-A-B6** has a positive influence on functioning of the skin and epidermal keratinisation. It affects the development and activity of sebaceous glands and improves the quality of coat. Administer in case of excessive hair loss, dry, dull and brittle hair. Due to their exceptional taste, the tablets are willingly consumed by animals.

###   Use

 Administer directly or mix with food: - cats and dogs: 1 tablet / 10 kg of body weight, twice a day for 3-4 weeks. The product contains protein of animal origin. It can be fed to animals other than ruminants.

###   Indications

 Delivery of zinc for for skin and fur.

###   for which animals

 For dogs and cats.

###   Ingredients

 Composition: dicalcium phosphate, by-products of animal origin (from pigs), brewer’s yeast (from Saccharomyces cerevisiae), maize starch (Zea mays), glycerin. Analytical constituents: crude protein 26,4%, crude fibre 5,8%, crude oils and fats 4,4%, crude ash 28,0%, phosphorus 5,5%, humidity 5,0%, hydrochlorides 0,4%. Additives in 1 kg: vitamins and provitamins: E672 vitamin A 1 600 000 IU, 3a831 vitamin B6 (pyridoxine hydrochloride) 122,9 mg, a mixture of trace elements: E6 zinc (zinc sulphate monohydrate) 25 000 mg.
