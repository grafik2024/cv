## purpose

**Hydra-Derm**  spray is a veterinary dermo-cosmetic. Juniper oil regulates sebum secretion and prevents excessive skin keratinisation. It relieves skin irritation. Softening effect of glycerine facilitates skin hydration, relieves itching and makes the coat shiny.

###   Use

 Local hydration and cleansing of skin with a tendency to seborrhoea. Softening the skin in case of localised excessive keratinisation (elbows , hocks , etc.). Keratotic and emollient action on the skin with symptoms caused by general inflammations e.g. allergies, ichthyosis.

###   Indications

 Spray the skin and coat in the affected area. Use every day or according to need.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions

 If product accidently gets into eyes, rinse thoroughly with warm water.

###   Ingredients

 Aqua, Glicerin, Polysorbate 20, Junipures Oxycedrus Wood Oil, Silicone Quaterium-17, Laureth-9, DMDM Hydantoin, Methylchloroisothiazolinone, Methylisothiazolinone
