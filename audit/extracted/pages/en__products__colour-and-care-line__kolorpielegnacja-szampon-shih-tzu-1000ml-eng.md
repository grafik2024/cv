## purpose

The shampoo is formulated for both washing properties and easy rinsing. Wheat proteins are very easily absorbed by the hair and skin. Suitable for dogs with dry and brittle coats. It leaves the coat clean and easy to comb. The coat is deeply nourished, giving it silkiness and shine.

###   Use

 For external use only. Shake well before use. Wet the coat, apply the shampoo, lather, wash and rinse. Repeat the action, leaving the shampoo on the fur and skin for a few minutes. Rinse with lukewarm water, dry and brush. After washing use the hydrating fluid HYDRA-DERM N.

###   Indications

 For long coat care. Long hair care.

###   for which animals

 For dogs.

###   Precautions

 Avoid contact with eyes.
